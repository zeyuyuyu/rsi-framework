\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
\paragraph{What the evidence does not support.} The improvement over the start clears its bar only on Spatial's unseen scenes, not on the official test of either suite nor on Goal's unseen scenes, and the self-repair margin misses its bar ($+2.7$ against $+3$). We do not claim that the loop beats plain RL at equal compute, nor that the language model's diagnosis, rather than the knob space it edits, carries the repair (its margin over blind search is $+1.2$ $[-0.6, +2.9]$).

\paragraph{For practitioners.} A fresh paired gate makes an unsafe recipe safe for one extra evaluation per candidate, but its strictness should match its noise; finals should be averaged, and freshly sampled test scenes should complement a fixed list of test states.

\paragraph{Limitations.} One simulated benchmark family, one 7B policy and a few-demonstration start; other policies and real robots are untested (real-robot data collection is in progress). Preregistration constrains the analysis, not the design: a different recipe space might leave more for a meta level to find.
