\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
The repair study shows how policy selection supports experimentation with the training program. Retained weights provide a common starting point for alternative programs, and selected changes guide later rounds. Binary task feedback thus informs both policy updates and the choice of how future experience will be used for learning.

Program adaptation also changes how compute is spent. The Goal comparison counts RL's pilot runs alongside training. The adapted loop combines policy learning with program search, then continues under the selected program. Its fresh-scene gains at lower recorded GPU cost motivate studying when to evaluate new program candidates and when to continue with the current program.
