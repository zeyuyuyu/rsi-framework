\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We built a recursive self-improvement framework in which every ingredient is a switch, preregistered every claim with a sealed test, and asked which part of the loop improves a VLA policy. The gate makes the loop safe; recursion and the verifier each contribute about a point and a half; a meta level that edits the procedure contributes nothing once the procedure works, for a reason we can show in the records; and in a second domain recursion pays only beyond four rounds. We also found that a published VLA-RL number owes about a fifth of itself to the benchmark's fixed test states. We hope the protocol---sealed tests, fresh placements, replicate-averaged finals, public amendments, read-once---is as useful to the community as the results, and that the self-repair study settles, one way or the other, when a meta level is worth its cost.
