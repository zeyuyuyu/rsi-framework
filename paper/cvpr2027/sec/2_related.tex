\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Learning from self-generated experience.} STaR~\cite{zelikman2022star}, ReST~\cite{gulcehre2023rest} and ReST-EM~\cite{singh2024restem} fine-tune language models on their own verified rationales. SPIN~\cite{chen2024spin} and self-rewarding models~\cite{yuan2024selfrewarding} also use model-generated outputs for further training; Absolute Zero~\cite{zhao2025absolutezero} generates tasks and verifies solutions through code execution. Group-relative policy optimisation is a common update rule for RL with verifiable rewards~\cite{shao2024deepseekmath,guo2025deepseekr1}. Our component comparisons examine how the source of this experience and its success labels contribute to repeated policy improvement. The on-policy analysis of~\cite{kim2026coverage} predicts early saturation with samples from a frozen initial policy, as observed in our twelve-round non-recursive control.

\paragraph{Adapting the training procedure.} LLM-driven search has been applied to programs and training configurations with large search spaces and inexpensive evaluation~\cite{novikov2025alphaevolve,lu2024aiscientist,hu2024adas}. Meta-Harness~\cite{lee2026metaharness} exposes earlier candidates' code, scores and traces; EvoTrainer~\cite{chen2026evotrainer} adds diagnostics and a memory of failed branches; LLMZero~\cite{fang2026llmzero} analyses signals at training checkpoints. Our workspace-based proposer also retains records for later proposals. We compare numeric and language-model proposals from working and failing programs, where each candidate requires policy training and rollout-based evaluation.

\paragraph{Robot learning and program improvement.} OpenVLA~\cite{kim2024openvla} and OpenVLA-OFT~\cite{kim2025oft} provide our policy family. SimpleVLA-RL~\cite{li2025simplevlarl}, RL4VLA~\cite{liu2025rl4vla} and related methods~\cite{lu2025vlarl} improve VLA policies through on-policy RL and binary success rewards. We use the SimpleVLA-RL stack and its released checkpoints. Concurrent approaches construct robot programs from execution feedback, including a code library requiring no model at run time~\cite{hu2026coap}, a validated skill hierarchy~\cite{wen2026roborsi}, and programs compiled from exploration~\cite{huang2026rax}. Our controlled comparisons separate improvements from adapting the training program and from updating policy weights.

\paragraph{Preregistered evaluation.} Preregistration specifies hypotheses and analyses before their results are observed~\cite{nosek2018preregistration,bertinetto2021preregistration}. We use it to specify comparisons, effect thresholds and analysis procedures.
