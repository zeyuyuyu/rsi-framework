\section{Decomposing the loop on LIBERO-Goal}
\label{sec:libero}

\paragraph{Setup.} LIBERO-Goal has ten goal-conditioned tasks in one scene. The starting policy is OpenVLA-OFT fine-tuned on one demonstration per task (pooled reference 58.9\% on the cloud platform and 58.4\% on the A800). The seed program, chosen from sealed pilots and frozen by amendment, is GRPO with learning rate $3\times10^{-5}$, matched-ratio log-probabilities, episode-balanced loss, 8 samples per scene at temperature 1.5, 160 scenes per inner step, 200 optimiser steps of batch 64 per update; 6 rounds of 3 inner steps; fresh paired gates. All 31 runs (seeds 1--3 for every arm, seed 0 added for the R2/R3/R5 arms) were read once on 2026-11-06 under the rule of \cref{sec:protocol}.

\paragraph{Results.} \Cref{tab:goal} gives the registered read and \cref{tab:arms} the per-arm finals. No preregistered bar is met. The loop's typical gain is one to two points: every gated run ends within noise of or above its start (finals 57.1--62.2 against a 58.4--58.9 reference; the two runs below their reference are within $-1.3$ and $-1.7$ points), and the best single seed gains $+3.3$ $[+0.1, +6.6]$. Recursion is the largest ingredient (R3, $+1.7$, CI just including 0; the preliminary single-evaluation read had passed the bar at $+2.4$ and did not survive replicate averaging), the verifier the next (R2, $+1.2$); without the verifier the loop ends exactly at its start (57.8--60.7). One round of plain RL with the same budget matches or beats the loop (R5 $-0.6$; on seed 0 the one-shot policy scores 62.7 against the gated loop's 57.2). All four meta-level arms are within $\pm 1.2$ points of \texttt{fixed} on both platforms.

\paragraph{What the gate buys.} The gate is what makes the loop safe rather than fast. In always-accept pilot curves on LIBERO-10, every recipe stayed within $\pm 3$ points of its start for 18 on-policy steps, and the behaviour-cloning recipe of \cref{sec:meta} collapsed from 58.5\% to 5.5\% on LIBERO-Goal when its versions were accepted unconditionally. Under the gate the same recipes never fall below their start on the sealed test. The price is that a 200-episode paired gate has a standard deviation of about 3.4 points, so it accepts and rejects many candidates on noise; acceptance rates are reported per run in the released records.

\paragraph{LIBERO-10 and LIBERO-Spatial.} On LIBERO-10 (long-horizon tasks, 19\% start) no sealed pilot recipe reached the preregistered $+3$ pooled-gate gain within 18 on-policy steps, so by the amendment's rule no main arms were run there and LIBERO-10 is reported as a negative result at this budget. On LIBERO-Spatial a single pilot curve rose from 59.5\% to 65.8\% (gate$+$audit, round 4); it was not continued to a sealed read.
