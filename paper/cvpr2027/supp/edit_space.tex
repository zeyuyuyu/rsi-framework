\paragraph{Edit spaces of the meta level.} The \emph{knob} space has ten hyperparameters: the number of instances per block, their allocation across tasks, attempts per instance, sampling temperature, the data rule and its budget, the update operator, learning rate, optimiser steps and batch size. The \emph{harness} space adds nine components of the procedure: the policy-gradient ratio, loss aggregation, BC likelihood temperature, advantage normalisation, the data band, a per-instance cap, an allocation floor and the two clip bounds.
