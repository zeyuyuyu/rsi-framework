# Pre-registration v3: recursive self-improvement of VLA policies on LIBERO (CVPR 2027 study)

Committed 2026-10-29, before any v3 run. Only two sealed pilots (tests never read) may run before the
seed programs are frozen in an addendum. Supersedes the v2 VLA study (LIBERO-goal, 50-episode gate), whose
remaining runs finish under VLA addendum 2 and are reported as the motivating negative result.

## Question

Does the RSI loop — the policy practises on placements it is assigned by its own curriculum, the simulator's
success flag is the only feedback, mixed-outcome experience updates a LoRA, a gate accepts or rejects each
round, and a meta level chooses the training program — make VLA policies of two different families better on
the sealed official LIBERO test, and is the gain due to the verifier, to recursion, to the meta level, and to
more than one big round of plain RL at equal compute?

| id | policy | start | suites |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M1 (headline) | OpenVLA-OFT (tokenized, chunk 8; SimpleVLA-RL's release) | 1-demo SFT per task (`Haozhan72/Openvla-oft-SFT-*-traj1`) | LIBERO-10 primary; spatial, object, goal secondary |

## Protocol (all models)

* **Training cases**: freshly sampled object placements every round (seeds 100_000_000 + ...). No official
  initial state is ever used for training, sampling, gating or program selection.
* **Gate**: 20 sampled placements per task (200 episodes); **audit**: 20 more per task (200), disjoint seeds;
  greedy, env re-seeded per episode, so versions are compared on identical placements.
* **Test (sealed)**: the official 50 initial states x 10 tasks (500 episodes), read only after the last round.
  Final versions are evaluated 3 times (the run's own test + 2 replicates, `run_libero --test-eval`); the
  initial reference is the per-episode mean over every in-run n0 test of that model and suite (VLA addendum 2).
* **Rounds**: 6. Acceptance: child gate >= parent gate. Up to 3 candidates per round for `full`.
* **Seeds**: 3 per arm for M1; 3 for M2 `fixed`/`full`, 2 for its controls if compute runs short (stated).

## Arms (per model, LIBERO-10)

| arm | what changes | isolates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fixed` | seed program every round, recursive | object-level RSI |
| `full` | meta level proposes programs (numeric proposer), 2 candidates/round | + meta level |
| `nonrec` | every round's experience generated by the initial policy | recursion |
| `shuffled` | success labels permuted within each case | the verifier |
| `onebig` | one round with the summed rollout and update budget of 6 rounds | iteration vs plain one-shot RL (SimpleVLA-RL-style under our protocol) |

## Bars (primary: LIBERO-10 sealed test, replicate-averaged, per model)

* **R1 improvement**: `full` and `fixed` final − initial >= +5.0 points for M1 and >= +3.0 for M2; paired
  bootstrap 95% CI over test episodes (clustered by initial state) excludes 0; >= 2 of 3 seeds individually
  exclude 0.
* **R2 verifier**: `fixed` − `shuffled` >= +3.0 points, CI excludes 0 (seed-averaged, per episode).
* **R3 recursion**: `fixed` − `nonrec` >= +2.0, CI excludes 0.
* **R4 meta**: `full` − `fixed` >= +2.0, CI excludes 0. Reported with the gate−test gap of the selected programs
  (winner's curse).
* **R5 vs one-shot RL**: best of `fixed`/`full` (chosen by gate, not test) − `onebig` >= +2.0, CI excludes 0.
* Noise check reported with every verdict: the same statistic between two halves of the n0 evaluations.
* Negative readings are reported as such.

## Secondary measurements (no bars)

* Other suites (M1 spatial/object/goal traj1; M2 goal), same arms where compute allows, `fixed` at least.
* **Train-on-test inflation**: SimpleVLA-RL's released LIBERO-10 RL checkpoint (trained on the 50 official
  initial states) evaluated on the official states and on 500 freshly sampled placements of the same tasks;
  the difference measures how much training on the test states inflates the published number. Our own finals
  get the same two evaluations.
* Robustness (if time): LIBERO-Plus / LIBERO-PRO perturbation sets, eval only.

## Pilots allowed before freezing

Two sealed 2-round pilots per model on LIBERO-10 (GRPO on mixed-outcome groups vs success-only BC), gate and
audit read, tests never read. They may set operator, lr, update steps and batch, curriculum size and sampling
temperature of the seed program; the choice and the reason go into addendum 1 before any main arm starts.
