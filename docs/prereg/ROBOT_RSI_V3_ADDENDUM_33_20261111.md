# Prereg v3, addendum 33: the guard-rail gate on the 6-round LIBERO-Spatial loop, against plain RL at equal compute

Committed 2026-11-11 ~16:30Z, before any run named here exists.

## Why

Every registered comparison of the gated loop with one round of plain RL at equal compute (`onebig`) has been a tie
or a loss:
- Goal at six rounds: R5 −0.58.
- Goal at twelve rounds: −0.38 on the official test, −2.73 on the fresh scenes.
- Spatial at six rounds, read today: R5 −0.45 official, F5 +0.38 fresh.

Addendum 32 traced the twelve-round loss to the strict gate, which throws away rounds whose dips are within its own noise. It registered a guard-rail gate on the 12-round Goal loop. Those three runs are at about round 5 of 12; only their gate-level round records have been seen, and no test of theirs exists. This addendum asks the same question on a second suite, at the six-round budget where `onebig` already exists.
