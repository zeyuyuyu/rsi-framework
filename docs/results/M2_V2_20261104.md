**Reading, as preregistered.** M2-A fails, so the paper claims a self-improvement loop, not a meta-level
contribution (addendum 21, "If M2-A fails"; addendum 14). Under this protocol the v2 meta level (an LLM that edits the
harness and sees per-task gates) does not beat a compute-matched fixed program. The pooled CI rules out a gain of
more than about +2 points on either start.

## Not claims (descriptive)

* **The loop itself.** Both arms end above their starting policy. On `pre`, n0 averages 58.8 over the six runs,
  the llm arms 61.1 and the rep arms 61.3. That is the object-level loop, not the meta level, and this study was
  not built to test it.
* **Test noise.** The same starting policy n0, evaluated on the same 500 episodes, scores between 57.2 and 60.0
  across the 12 runs (sd ≈ 0.7). The evaluation itself carries about ±1.5 points of run-to-run noise, of the same
  order as every per-seed difference above.
