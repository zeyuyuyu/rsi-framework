## What the read says

1. **R3-long passes, but not for the predicted reason.** The prediction (addendum 27) was that `fixed12` would gain
   1-3 points over its 6-round finals while `nonrec12` stayed where `nonrec` had ended. Neither happened: `fixed12`
   ends where `fixed` ended (−0.09), and `nonrec12` ends 6.4 points *below* `nonrec` and 4-11 points below its own
   start on every seed. The +7.8-point separation is the non-recursive loop degrading, not the recursive loop
   improving. The registered claim ("recursion separates at the longer horizon") is met by its definition; the paper
   states the mechanism as observed.
2. **The gate did not prevent the degradation.** Every `nonrec12` version was accepted by a fresh paired gate
   (candidate ≥ parent on 200 never-seen placements), yet the retained policies are worse than the initial policy on
   the sealed test by 4-11 points. With one candidate per round, acceptance on `≥`, and a candidate that is truly a
   little worse than its parent (practice generated by a policy the learner has drifted away from), noise-driven
   acceptances accumulate over twelve rounds. The 6-round safety finding ("no gated run ends below its start beyond
   noise", 31 runs) therefore holds at six rounds and for recursive practice; it does **not** hold at twelve rounds
   for the non-recursive arm. The paper's safety claim is narrowed accordingly.
3. **R5-long fails, as the honest prior said.** One round of 36 on-policy steps from the initial policy ties the
   12-round gated loop (−0.38); `onebig12`'s single candidate was kept by the gate on all three seeds, so the
   ungated sensitivity read is identical. The claim "the gated recursive loop beats one-shot RL at equal compute"
   is not made.
4. **R1-long fails.** `fixed12` is +1.6 points over the reference, the same one-to-two-point gain as at six rounds;
   only one seed is individually above zero.
5. **Noise check.** The two halves of the nine n0 evaluations differ by −0.85 [−1.83, +0.08]; the R3-long effect is
   an order of magnitude larger than this, R5-long and R1-long are within it.
