## 1. The framework in one paragraph

A policy practises on freshly sampled scenes, an external oracle (the simulator's success flag, or a sandboxed
interpreter) labels every attempt, the verified experience updates a LoRA (GRPO / BC / SFT), and the improved policy
generates the next round's practice (**recursion**). A **gate** keeps a candidate only if it does not regress on 200
fresh, paired held-out placements; an **audit** set is recorded but never decides; the official **test** stays
sealed until every decision of a study is made. A **meta level** versions the training program (JSON) and lets a
numeric search or an LLM edit it inside a declared space; a program is credited only when its child wins the gate.
Every control is a switch in the same engine: `nonrec` (practice generated by the frozen initial policy),
`shuffled` (success labels permuted), `onebig` (the whole budget in one round of plain RL), `fixed` / `full` / `llm`
(meta level off / numeric / LLM). See `README.md` and `rsi/core/engine.py`.

## 2. Protocol (robot study, prereg v3 + 30 amendments)

* Policy: OpenVLA-OFT 7B started from a one-demonstration-per-task SFT (`Haozhan72/Openvla-oft-SFT-libero-goal-traj1`,
  about 59% on LIBERO-Goal). Training stack: SimpleVLA-RL rollouts + LoRA (r=32).
* Seed program (frozen by amendment after sealed pilots): GRPO, lr 3e-5, matched ratio, episode-balanced loss,
  8 samples per scene at temperature 1.5, 160 scenes per inner step, 200 optimiser steps of batch 64; 6 rounds of
  3 inner steps; fresh paired gates (20 placements per task).
* Sealed test: the official 50 initial states × 10 tasks. Final of a run = per-episode mean of three evaluations of
  the retained policy (its own test + two `--test-eval --replicates 2` jobs). Initial reference = per-episode mean of
  every in-run n0 evaluation on that platform (Yangquan 36 evaluations, 58.92%; A800 21, 58.44%).
* Statistics: seed-averaged per-episode paired bootstrap, 10,000 draws, 95% CI; a noise check (two halves of the n0
  evaluations) next to every verdict. Reader: `scripts/report/goal_read.py --mode final`.
* Two platforms (Yangquan cloud pool, A800 node) with non-identical evaluation stacks: comparisons are paired within
  a platform; the platforms replicate each other.

### 4.1b LIBERO-Goal, 12 rounds (addendum 27, read 2026-11-08): R3-long passes, R5-long and R1-long fail

`fixed12` (12 rounds, recursive) vs `nonrec12` (practice from the frozen initial policy) vs `onebig12` (one round of
36 steps), Yangquan seeds 1-3, replicate-averaged finals, pooled reference 58.93%. Full note: `docs/results/LONG12_READ_20261108.md`.

| claim | comparison | points | 95% CI | bar | result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R3-long | fixed12 − nonrec12 | +7.84 | [+5.22, +10.49] | +2 | passes (3/3 seeds > 0) |
| R5-long | fixed12 − onebig12 | −0.38 | [−2.42, +1.64] | +2 | fails |
| R1-long | fixed12 − reference | +1.60 | [−0.88, +4.13] | +5 | fails |
| descriptive | fixed12 − fixed (6 rounds) | −0.09 | [−1.98, +1.78] | | no gain from six more rounds |
| descriptive | nonrec12 − nonrec (6 rounds) | −6.44 | [−9.00, −3.96] | | the non-recursive loop degraded |

Reading: the separation is the non-recursive loop *degrading* (4-11 points below its start on every seed, every
version accepted by a fresh paired gate), not the recursive loop improving (it ends where it ended at six rounds).
Two consequences for the claims: recursion is necessary to keep a gated loop from drifting down over twelve rounds,
and the 6-round safety finding ("no gated run ends below its start") is narrowed to six rounds / recursive practice.
One-shot RL at equal compute still ties the loop.

### 4.2 Meta level v2: null on both platforms

M2-A llm − replica: −0.27 [−2.53, +2.00] (Yangquan), +0.30 [−1.60, +2.15] (A800); M2-B +0.47 [−1.80, +2.73].
Mechanism (add. 23, gate data only): LLM proposals score −1.2 against the incumbent at the gate (n=30) while
identical-program replicas score +0.6 (sd 3.4, n=36); the LLM's diagnoses are correct (unsolved task, saturated task,
changes within noise) but the object loop reaches the same plateau under every program in the space (final gates
62.8 / 63.5 / 65.0 / 61.7 for fixed / full / llm_harness / onebig).

## 6. What the evidence supports, by strength

* **Significant (CI excludes 0):** the gated loop does not degrade at six rounds with recursive practice (31/31 runs; the same recipe collapses to 5.5%
  without the gate); verified feedback improves code world models (+2.6 to +5.8); recursion helps at 8 rounds in the
  code domain (+2.8, replicated on 7B); one VLA seed +3.3 [+0.1, +6.6]; train-on-test inflation +16.8.
* **Significant but under its bar:** self-repair S1, llm_harness − fixed from a failing recipe, +2.65 [+0.73, +4.58] (bar +3).
* **Directional (positive point estimate, CI includes 0, bar missed):** VLA loop +1.7, recursion +1.7, verifier +1.2; self-repair S2 (LLM − numeric search) +1.15.
* **Null:** meta level on a working recipe (±1.2; v2 −0.3 / +0.3, mechanism in 4.2); loop vs one-shot RL (−0.6); self-repaired loop vs the hand-tuned loop (S3 +0.3).
* **Pending:** the LIBERO-Spatial main study (addendum 28, 16 runs launched 2026-11-10; the Goal arms on a second suite) and the self-repair study on Spatial with meta level v3 (addendum 29, 15 runs). Every earlier registered read is done (2026-11-09).

## 7. Operational disclosures that affect reading

* Round-boundary continuations with more rollout GPUs (same plan.json, `--resume`; the proposer's random stream is
  re-seeded on resume) for the slowest self-repair arms, and one failed continuation job that touched no data:
  addendum 26 operational notes. Addendum 25: the `onebig_s0` crash/resume and GPU contention.
* The 2026-11-06 Feishu report's claim "fixed arm refused all six rounds" was wrong (only seed 2's fixed arm is held at
  n0). Its claim "all three LLM seeds switched BC to GRPO" holds for seeds 1, 2 and A800 seed 0 (kept by the gate) but
  not seed 3, where the switch was refused and the LLM repaired BC instead; a first correction on 11-07 overstated
  this. The table in section 5 and `docs/CASE_STUDIES.md` are the record.
